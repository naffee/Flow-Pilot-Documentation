# AI Workflow Automation

FlowPilot's core strength lies in its ability to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) directly into your workflows. This allows you to process unstructured data and make intelligent decisions automatically.

## AI Actions

Our platform supports a variety of AI-driven actions:

### Text Summarization
Condense long emails, reports, or articles into concise summaries. You can specify the desired length (Short, Medium, Long) and format (Bullet points, Paragraph).

### Sentiment Analysis
Automatically detect the sentiment of incoming customer support tickets or social media mentions. Route negative feedback to specialized teams for immediate attention.

### Content Generation
Draft blog posts, marketing copy, or email responses based on keywords or prompts.

## Configuration

To configure an AI step:

1. Select **FlowPilot AI** from the app menu.
2. Choose your desired action.
3. Map input data from previous steps.
4. Select the model (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, etc.) based on your plan tier.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Data Privacy:** FlowPilot does not train models on your data. All data sent to AI providers is encrypted in transit and at rest.
