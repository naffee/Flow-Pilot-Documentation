# Managing Your Subscription

FlowPilot offers flexible plans to suit your automation needs.

## Upgrading Your Plan

To upgrade your subscription:

1. Navigate to **Account Settings** > **Billing**.
2. Click **Change Plan**.
3. Select the desired tier (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise).
4. Review the prorated charges and confirm.

## Downgrading or Canceling

You can downgrade or cancel your subscription at any time.

> [!WARNING]
> Downgrading to a lower tier may result in the loss of access to premium features like advanced AI models or increased task limits.

To cancel:
1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing cycle.
