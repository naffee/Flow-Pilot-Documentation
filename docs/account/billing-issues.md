# Payment & Billing Issues

Resolving common payment problems.

## Updating Payment Method

If your credit card has expired or you wish to change your payment method:

1. Go to **Billing** > **Payment Methods**.
2. Click **Add New Card**.
3. Set the new card as the default method.
4. Remove the old card if desired.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, we will attempt to charge your card again over the next 3 days. If payment still fails after retries, your account may be temporarily paused.

## Invoices

Download past invoices for your records by visiting the **Billing History** section. Invoices are available as PDF downloads.
