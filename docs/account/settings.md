# Updating Profile Settings

Keep your account information up to date.

## Personal Information

You can update your name, email address, and password from the **Profile** tab in settings.

## Notification Preferences

Manage how and when you receive alerts from FlowPilot.

- **Email Digests:** Receive a daily or weekly summary of workflow activity.
- **System Alerts:** Get notified immediately if a critical workflow fails.
- **Product Updates:** opt-in to hear about new features and improvements.

## Security

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for added security. We support authenticator apps like Google Authenticator and Authy.
