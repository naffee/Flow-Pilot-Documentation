# Introduction to FlowPilot
{: .no_toc }

FlowPilot is an AI-powered workflow automation platform designed to help teams orchestrate complex business processes with ease. By combining a visual workflow builder with advanced large language models (LLMs), FlowPilot enables you to automate tasks that previously required human intervention.

## What is FlowPilot?

FlowPilot serves as a bridge between your operational data and artificial intelligence. It allows you to:

- **Connect Apps:** Integrate with over 200+ SaaS tools including Slack, Salesforce, Jira, and Google Workspace.
- **Automate Logic:** Use visual logic nodes to define conditions, loops, and branching paths.
- **Deploy AI Agents:** Embed AI steps that can summarize text, generate content, analyze sentiment, or make decisions based on data.

## Key Concepts

### Workflows
A **Workflow** is an automated process consisting of a trigger and a series of actions. Think of it as a recipe for automation.

### Triggers
Every workflow starts with a **Trigger**. This could be an event in another app (e.g., "New Email Received"), a schedule (e.g., "Every Monday at 9 AM"), or a manual webhook.

### Actions
**Actions** are the steps performed after a trigger. These can be standard operations like "Send Slack Message" or AI-driven tasks like "Draft Response."
