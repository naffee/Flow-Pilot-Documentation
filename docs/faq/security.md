# Data Security

Security is our top priority at FlowPilot.

## Compliance

We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR ready.

## Data Encryption

- **At Rest:** AES-256 encryption.
- **In Transit:** TLS 1.3 encryption for all data transmission.

## Access Control

We support Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML and OIDC for Enterprise plans, allowing you to manage access through Okta, OneLogin, or Google Workspace.

## AI Safety

We have strict agreements with our AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) ensuring that your data is **never used for model training**.
